# Admissions

> **Illustrative demo data.** These are example steps for a fictional college,
> not real admission rules.

## How to Apply for Admission

Follow these example steps to apply:

1. **Check eligibility** for your chosen program (see Eligibility below).
2. **Fill in the online application form** on the demo admissions portal.
3. **Upload documents:** mark sheets, a photo ID, and a passport-size photograph.
4. **Pay the application fee** of 500 (demo currency units).
5. **Attend counselling** on the date shown in your confirmation email.
6. **Confirm admission** by paying the first-semester fee before the deadline.

## Eligibility

- **B.Tech programs:** Pass in Class 12 (or equivalent) with Physics and Mathematics.
- **BBA program:** Pass in Class 12 (or equivalent) in any stream.

## Important Admission Dates

In this demo, applications open on **1 May** and close on **30 June** each year.
Counselling takes place in **July**.

## Scholarships

Merit scholarships are available for students in the top 5% of applicants in this demo.
Students can ask about scholarships at the Admissions Office.
