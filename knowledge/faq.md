# Frequently Asked Questions

> **Illustrative demo data** for a fictional college.

## How do I contact the college?

- **Email:** info@greenfield-demo.example
- **Phone:** +00 0000 000000 (placeholder number for the demo)
- **In person:** Visit the Administrative Office during office hours.

## What if the chatbot cannot answer my question?

If the chatbot cannot find an answer in its knowledge files, please:

1. Email your question to info@greenfield-demo.example, or
2. Visit the Administrative Office during office hours.

A staff member will reply within two working days in this demo.

## Is there a dress code?

Students are expected to wear neat, comfortable clothing. Laboratory sessions
require closed shoes and a lab coat.

## Can I change my program after admission?

Program changes may be requested within the first two weeks of the first semester,
subject to seat availability.
