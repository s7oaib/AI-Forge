# Departments and Academic Programs

> **Illustrative demo data** for a fictional college. Edit this file to change what the chatbot knows.

## Departments Available

Greenfield Demo College has five academic departments. Each department offers one
undergraduate degree program (course) that students can choose from:

1. **Computer Science and Engineering** - B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering
2. **Electronics and Communication Engineering** - B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering
3. **Mechanical Engineering** - B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering
4. **Civil Engineering** - B.Tech in Civil Engineering
5. **Business Administration** - Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)

## Program Duration

- B.Tech programs last **4 years** (8 semesters).
- The BBA program lasts **3 years** (6 semesters).

## Intake per Program

Each B.Tech program admits up to 60 students per year in this demo. The BBA program
admits up to 40 students per year.
