# College Overview

> **Illustrative demo data.** Greenfield Demo College is a fictional institution
> created for a chatbot workshop. None of these facts describe a real college.

## About Greenfield Demo College

Greenfield Demo College is a fictional, co-educational college used to demonstrate
how a chatbot answers questions from approved knowledge files. The demo campus is
described as being located in "Greenfield City" (also fictional).

## Vision and Mission

- **Vision:** To be a welcoming place for learning, curiosity, and responsible use of technology.
- **Mission:** To offer practical, project-based education and support every student's growth.

## Academic Calendar

The demo academic year is divided into two semesters:

- **Odd semester:** August to December
- **Even semester:** January to May

Summer break runs from June to July.

## Office Hours

The College Administrative Office is open:

- **Monday to Friday:** 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- **Saturday:** 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM
- **Sunday and public holidays:** Closed

The office handles certificates, ID cards, fee receipts, and general enquiries.
