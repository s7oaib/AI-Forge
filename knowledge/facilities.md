# Campus Facilities

> **Illustrative demo data** for a fictional college.

## Facilities Overview

The demo campus offers the following facilities:

- **Central Library** with printed books, journals, and a digital reading room
- **Computer Labs** with internet access for programming and project work
- **Science and Engineering Labs** for practical sessions
- **Sports Complex** with a cricket ground, basketball court, and indoor games room
- **Cafeteria** serving vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals
- **Hostels** with separate blocks for male and female students
- **Medical Room** with a nurse on duty during college hours
- **Wi-Fi** across academic buildings

## Library Hours

The Central Library is open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
It is closed on Sundays. Students need a valid college ID card to borrow books.

## Hostel Accommodation

Hostel rooms are shared by two or three students. Hostel applications are made
after admission is confirmed, through the Hostel Office.

## Transport

College buses run on four routes within Greenfield City in this demo.
Bus passes are issued by the Administrative Office.
